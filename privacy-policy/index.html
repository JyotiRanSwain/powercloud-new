We process your data only with your free, specific, informed and unambiguous consent, given by ticking the consent box on each form. We do not sell your personal data. Links to WhatsApp, YouTube, LinkedIn and Instagram open third-party services with their own privacy policies. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.

5. Security

We take reasonable security safeguards: encrypted (HTTPS) connections, access limited to authorised PowerCloud personnel, a private storage folder for CVs, and regular deletion of data after the retention period. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

6. Your rights as a Data Principal

  • Get a summary of the personal data we hold and how it is used.
  • Correct, complete or update your data.
  • Ask us to erase your data.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Seek grievance redressal.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.

To exercise any right, use the Data Rights & Grievance page or email info@powercloud.in. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
